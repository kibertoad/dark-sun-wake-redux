# cast_cure

A function: the rest's cast of one priest spell from a caster on a target at a caster level through the hit routine overlay 179 `+0C7F` in BLD-GOG-EN-1.1, with `DS:0DAB` set to 5 for the call and spending nothing from the spell pools [FND-MAGIC-005]. Cure Disease (148) and Neutralize Poison (178) remove every effect of code 27 and 71; Remove Curse (157) raises current Strength to base Strength; Remove Paralysis (159) removes effects 91, 70 and 65 to 68, and 69 unless the target has effect 71; the level changes nothing [FND-MAGIC-021]. No other build has been described.
