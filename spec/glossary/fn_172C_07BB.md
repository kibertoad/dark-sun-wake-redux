(FND-SCRIPT-021). It selects the first slot with the largest signed age
among the 16 slots, sets its start, end and number words to FFFF and its
age to signed minus one, then returns the zero-extended slot index. It
leaves the selector and script-buffer bytes unchanged. The fill body
uses it when no free start exists; the allocator can also call it.

Q-SCRIPT-003. No other build has been described.
