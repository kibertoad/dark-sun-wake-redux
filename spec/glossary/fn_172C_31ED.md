# fn_172C_31ED

The near helper at `172C:31ED` in BLD-GOG-EN-1.1, called at script entry
and on the loader path after its early returns. When byte `4C0E:0000`
equals one it requests zero fills of 9, 64, 40 and 420 bytes through the
far pointers at `4C13:033B`, `0337`, `0333` and `032F`; otherwise it
returns without fills [FND-SCRIPT-020]. The four buffers are
`local_flags`, `local_numbers`, `local_big_numbers` and `local_strings`
with their full allocated sizes, and the byte is 1 from the end of the
script set-up on [FND-SCRIPT-026]. No other build has been described.
