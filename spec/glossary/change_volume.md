# change_volume

The adjustment the manual gives the music and sound-effects volume arrows of the Preferences screen. In BLD-GOG-EN-1.1 the inspected Preferences arrows adjust message delay and effects volume; a general absence of music-level controls is unproved [FND-CONFIG-235], and the sound-effects arrows change the byte RULE-CONFIG-004 describes [FND-CONFIG-010]. No rule defines a function by this name. No other build has been described.
