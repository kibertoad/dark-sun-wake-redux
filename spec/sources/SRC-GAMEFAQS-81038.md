## Use

A community walkthrough built from play. It gives the route through the game,
the quest branches, the outcomes the author saw for choices, mechanics the
author worked out by experiment, and reported bugs, soft locks and
disagreements with the manual. It does not say which version or release the
author played, so every claim it makes is a lead until a run of
BLD-GOG-EN-1.1 or a reading of the executable confirms it.

## Known errors

the sources say section of each rule that relies on this source.
