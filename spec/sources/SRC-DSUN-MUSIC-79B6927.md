## Use

Programs and notes that read the GFF container, its indexed images and
palettes, region data and XMI music. They identify resource types and give
field layouts. They were written for the Dark Sun games in general and do not
name the release they were tested on, so each layout is a lead to check
against the files of BLD-GOG-EN-1.1.
