# EXPLORE

| Spec ID | Title | Spec status | Code | Tests | Deviations | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RULE-EXPLORE-001` | Holding the pointer at an edge of the map view scrolls the view toward that edge until the pointer leaves it or the map ends | sourced | partial | None | None | sourced | `ExplorationInput` sends a one-pixel `Scroll` command while the pointer is on the outermost row or column of the logical canvas, both axes at a corner, and `ExplorationSession` clamps the camera to the region. The band width, step and rate are the rule's open questions, so these are the rebuild's choices. Center on Leader targets the leader's visual centre, also a choice of the rebuild. |
| `RULE-EXPLORE-002` | Key 5 shows the whole party on the map and key 6, outside combat, shows only the leader | supported | partial | None | None | supported | `ExplorationHotkeys` maps `5` and `6` to `ShowExpandedParty` and `ShowLeaderOnly`, and `ExplorationSession` keeps the display mode, starting with the leader alone. Key 6 is not refused in combat, no member is put on or taken off the map, and no cells are freed. |
| `RULE-EXPLORE-003` | Objects occupy the map cells of their footprint by setting the cells' blocked and occupied bits, which a cell test for movement reads | supported | partial | None | None | supported | `RegionTerrainGrid` reads `blocked` as a movement block, treats cells outside the map as blocked and keeps every cell's byte. `ExplorationOccupancySession` places, moves and removes caller-given footprints atomically, keeping occupants in its own state instead of the cell bits, and `GridPathfinder` routes around them under COMPAT-PATH-001. The opening leader gets a single cell at `(74,91)`, where FND-EXPLORE-003 gives `(74,93)`. Footprint sizes from the details byte, the 21-cell area, the first-cell bookkeeping and the party cells are not implemented. |
| `RULE-EXPLORE-004` | The keypad direction keys step the chosen character one cell, or in combat attack the object whose area holds the blocked cell | supported | missing | None | None | supported | The rebuild has no keypad movement. |
